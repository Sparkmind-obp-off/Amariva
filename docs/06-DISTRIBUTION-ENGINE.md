# AMARIVA — DISTRIBUTION ENGINE

## Principle

Social channels are distribution surfaces, not the business itself.

The objective is to move attention into owned or measurable assets:

**social → AMARIVA page/tool → usage → lead → offer**

## Channel Roles

### Search
High intent, compounding discovery.

### Instagram / TikTok
Demonstration, short utility, before/after, simple education.

### YouTube
Deep explanation, walkthroughs, searchable demonstrations.

### LinkedIn
B2B credibility, operator insights, product reasoning.

### X / Threads
Fast demand discovery, conversation, testing language, distribution.

### Email
Owned retention and launch channel.

Use only the channels that match the audience and content.

## Distribution Content Types

### Demonstration
Show the tool solving a real problem.

### Insight
State a useful observation.

### Mistake
Explain an expensive/annoying error.

### Before / After
Show operational improvement.

### Question
Turn a real demand signal into a discussion.

### Proof
Show actual outputs, examples, or outcomes.

## CTA Design

Avoid generic:

“Learn more.”

Prefer:

- “Calculate yours”
- “Check your score”
- “Generate the template”
- “Try the free tool”
- “See the full workflow”

## Distribution Loop

1. Observe demand.
2. Publish a useful answer.
3. Route to tool/content.
4. Measure clicks and completion.
5. Learn which language converts.
6. Update product/page.
7. Republish the stronger version.

## Owned Audience

Build email capture around utility:

- saved result,
- optional report,
- update notification,
- new tool alert,
- product education.

Never make email signup the price of basic usefulness unless there is a clear reason.

## Community Strategy

Respond to real questions.

Do not spam links.

The strongest distribution signal is:

> people independently repeating the problem language the product solves.
