# AMARIVA — PRODUCT & MONETIZATION

## Product Philosophy

AMARIVA products should turn repeated problems into repeatable solutions.

The product should become more valuable than the free content around it.

## Product Validation Ladder

Before building full software:

**Demand signal → manual prototype → simple digital product → repeat transactions → workflow product → software**

Do not build complex SaaS from assumptions.

## Product Types

### Digital
Templates, kits, guides, calculators, libraries.

### Workflow
Structured processes, reusable operating systems.

### Software
Repeated-use workflows delivered through a web product.

### Solution
Standardized implementation around a core product.

## Monetization Logic

Free assets answer:

> “Can I solve the first part?”

Paid assets answer:

> “Can I solve the whole problem faster and better?”

Recurring software answers:

> “Can this keep doing the work for me?”

## Product Page Structure

1. Problem.
2. Desired outcome.
3. What the product does.
4. What is included.
5. Demonstration.
6. Proof.
7. Pricing.
8. FAQ.
9. Risk reducer.
10. CTA.

## Revenue Portfolio Rule

Avoid depending on a single product.

Target portfolio over time:

- acquisition products,
- core products,
- recurring products,
- expansion products.

However, portfolio breadth should follow proven demand.

## Margin Discipline

For every paid product track:

**revenue − payment fees − infrastructure − fulfillment − support − acquisition cost = contribution**

A product that sells but consumes disproportionate support time is not automatically healthy.

## Product Analytics

Track:

- viewed,
- started,
- activated,
- purchased,
- delivered,
- returned,
- upgraded,
- refunded,
- referred.

## Build/Buy/Partner

Build when the workflow is strategically differentiating.

Buy/use a third-party service when infrastructure is commodity.

Partner when distribution or specialized capability would take too long to recreate.

Keep the public customer experience under AMARIVA regardless of backend provider where practical.
