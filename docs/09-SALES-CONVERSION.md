# AMARIVA — SALES & CONVERSION

## Sales Philosophy

AMARIVA should be primarily **product-led**.

Sales should help the buyer make a decision, not pressure the buyer into one.

## Conversion Sequence

**Problem clarity → desired outcome → proof → offer clarity → low-friction CTA → payment → immediate value**

## Conversion Assets

Priority order:

1. Product page
2. Interactive demo / free tool
3. Proof / case evidence
4. FAQ
5. Comparison
6. Checkout
7. Post-purchase onboarding

## CTA Hierarchy

Primary CTA:
**start/use/buy the relevant next step**

Secondary CTA:
**learn/compare/save**

Avoid multiple equal CTAs.

## Sales-Assisted Layer

Use human contact when:

- the product is high value,
- implementation requires context,
- there is a procurement decision,
- a buyer asks for clarification.

Do not create a sales team before the demand signal requires it.

## Lead Qualification

Capture only what improves the next decision.

Possible fields:

- problem,
- business type,
- current workflow,
- desired outcome,
- urgency,
- selected product.

Do not build a giant CRM form for a simple purchase.

## Checkout Friction

Reduce:

- unnecessary steps,
- confusing pricing,
- surprise fees,
- unclear fulfillment,
- unsupported payment expectations.

The buyer should know what happens next before paying.

## Trust

Use:

- clear identity,
- honest limitations,
- visible terms,
- privacy explanation,
- support path,
- real examples,
- transparent pricing.

## Conversion Experiments

Test one major variable at a time:

- headline,
- CTA,
- proof placement,
- pricing framing,
- demo,
- offer bundle.

Do not change everything simultaneously; otherwise learning is weak.
