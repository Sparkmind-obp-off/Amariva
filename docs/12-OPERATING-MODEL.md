# AMARIVA — OPERATING MODEL

## Operating Principle

AMARIVA should operate like a small product company with a disciplined growth loop, not like an agency waiting for custom work.

## Core Functions

### Demand
Find real problems and intent.

### Product
Turn problems into useful free and paid assets.

### Distribution
Move useful ideas into search and social channels.

### Commerce
Convert demand into transactions.

### Delivery
Deliver the promised result.

### Intelligence
Measure outcomes and prioritize next actions.

## Weekly Operating Cycle

### Monday — Demand
Review questions, searches, conversations, behavior.

### Tuesday — Build
Improve one tool/product or build a validated next asset.

### Wednesday — Content
Publish one substantive piece and derivative distribution content.

### Thursday — Conversion
Improve a landing page, offer, checkout, or onboarding flow.

### Friday — Intelligence
Review metrics, experiments, economics, and next actions.

The exact schedule can change; the loop should remain.

## Priority Queue

Every task should be classified:

**P0 — Revenue/blocker**

**P1 — Demand/activation**

**P2 — Product quality**

**P3 — Nice-to-have**

Do not let design work displace revenue or learning-critical work without reason.

## One Metric, One Owner

Every initiative needs:

- objective,
- metric,
- owner,
- deadline,
- decision rule.

## Documentation Rule

Each major asset should have:

- owner,
- purpose,
- audience,
- source demand,
- CTA,
- linked offer,
- success metric,
- last reviewed date.

## Automation Principle

Automate after a workflow is understood.

Do not automate confusion.

Start manually when needed, instrument it, then automate repeatable work.

## Business Health Check

At least monthly review:

- top acquisition assets,
- top converting tools,
- top products,
- revenue concentration,
- retention,
- support load,
- infrastructure cost,
- experiment velocity.

## Strategic Constraint

AMARIVA must remain simple enough to operate by a small team.

Complexity is a cost.
