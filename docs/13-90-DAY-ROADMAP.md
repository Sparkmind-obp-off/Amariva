# AMARIVA — 90-DAY LAUNCH ROADMAP

## Objective

Build the first working demand-to-revenue loop, not a giant product catalog.

---

## PHASE 0 — FOUNDATION
### Days 1–7

Lock:

- brand architecture,
- public messaging,
- domain strategy,
- basic identity,
- analytics conventions,
- legal/policy baseline,
- repository/documentation structure.

Deliverable:
**AMARIVA public foundation ready.**

---

## PHASE 1 — FIRST DEMAND CLUSTER
### Days 8–21

Choose one problem cluster using real demand signals.

Create:

- 1 flagship free tool,
- 3–5 supporting content pages,
- 1 lead capture path,
- 1 relevant entry paid product.

Deliverable:
**first end-to-end funnel.**

---

## PHASE 2 — FIRST TRAFFIC ENGINE
### Days 22–35

Launch:

- search-focused pages,
- short distribution content,
- tool demonstrations,
- internal links,
- measurable CTAs.

Start collecting:

- source,
- tool usage,
- CTA clicks,
- leads,
- purchases.

Deliverable:
**repeatable acquisition loop.**

---

## PHASE 3 — CONVERSION OPTIMIZATION
### Days 36–50

Improve:

- headline,
- tool UX,
- result explanation,
- proof,
- pricing,
- checkout,
- onboarding.

Deliverable:
**known conversion bottleneck + experiment log.**

---

## PHASE 4 — PRODUCT DEPTH
### Days 51–65

Improve the winning problem cluster.

Potentially add:

- second free tool,
- stronger entry product,
- deeper core product,
- automation or software prototype.

Deliverable:
**one validated product ladder.**

---

## PHASE 5 — RETENTION
### Days 66–80

Implement:

- onboarding,
- saved results,
- email/update path,
- post-purchase education,
- cross-sell logic.

Deliverable:
**repeat usage or repeat-purchase mechanism.**

---

## PHASE 6 — SCALE WHAT WORKS
### Days 81–90

Review:

- top traffic sources,
- highest-intent keywords/topics,
- best free tool,
- best offer,
- best conversion path,
- customer feedback,
- contribution economics.

Then decide:

**double down / iterate / kill.**

Deliverable:
**next 90-day growth thesis backed by actual data.**

---

## 90-DAY SUCCESS DEFINITION

Success is NOT:

- publishing many pages,
- opening many social accounts,
- having a beautiful homepage,
- collecting followers.

Success is:

> **At least one repeatable path from discovery → useful free value → commercial intent → paid transaction → measurable learning.**
